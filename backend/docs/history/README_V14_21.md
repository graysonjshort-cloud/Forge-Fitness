# Forge Fitness v14.21 — AI Daily Nutrition Coaching

Adds daily nutrition intelligence to Forge Coach.

- Answers how many calories/protein/carbs/fat are logged and remaining today.
- Uses actual Nutrition-tab targets and entries, not invented user data.
- Suggests what type of meal/snack best fits remaining macros.
- Prefers previously confirmed Saved Foods when one fits the remaining targets.
- Adds Today's Nutrition and Meal Idea quick prompts in Coach.
- Adds a Daily Nutrition Check card to the Nutrition tab with direct Coach shortcuts.
- Existing Smart Food Logging and v14.20 saved-food reuse remain intact.
