# Forge Fitness v14.10 — Dynamic Workout Names

Workout names now reflect training focus and sport context instead of generic A/B labels.

Examples:
- Upper Strength
- Upper Volume
- Lower Strength
- Lower Volume
- Push Strength
- Pull Volume
- Full Body Power
- Basketball — Lower Body Power
- Soccer — Strength & Power
- Swimming — Upper Body Performance

The underlying split and exercise programming remain intact; only the user-facing workout titles are made clearer and more specific.
