# Forge Fitness v14.10.1 — Clear Workout Names

Reworked workout titles to be immediately understandable.

Examples:
- Upper Body
- Lower Body
- Upper Body 2
- Lower Body 2
- Chest, Shoulders & Triceps
- Back & Biceps
- Legs
- Full Body
- Lower Body Strength & Power
- Full Body Strength & Conditioning

Sport names are no longer unnecessarily prefixed onto every workout. Sport-specific wording is used only when it helps explain what the workout actually focuses on.
