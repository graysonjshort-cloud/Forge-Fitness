# Forge Fitness v14.14 — Swappable Cardio + Intensity

Cardio add-ons are now swappable from the Workout screen.

The swap list only shows cardio exercises compatible with the user's Equipment Log.
Swaps persist to the active plan and keep the current cardio duration/intensity.

Cardio Intensity is now a separate setting:
- Light: 10 minutes and favors steady-state cardio.
- Moderate: 15 minutes with balanced/sport-aware selection.
- High: 20 minutes and favors interval cardio.

Cardio Frequency still controls how many regular workouts per week get cardio.
Intensity and Frequency can both be changed during onboarding and later in Training Settings.
