# Forge Articulated Demo Standard v2

v14.36.9 pauses library expansion and upgrades the instructional quality of the five reference movements.

## Required visual elements
- Defined torso rather than a single stick line.
- Visible shoulder/elbow/hip/knee articulation where relevant.
- Exercise-specific equipment geometry.
- Red movement reference or bar path.
- Controlled eccentric, brief working-position hold, controlled concentric/return.
- Short on-canvas technique cue.
- Dark Forge background, silver athlete, red movement/equipment accent.

## Reference movements upgraded
- Back Squat
- Barbell Bench Press
- Romanian Deadlift
- Barbell Overhead Press
- Pull-Up

These are still diagrams rather than photorealistic anatomy. None are automatically biomechanically certified.
Every V2 asset remains Asset Ready until all six review gates are passed in Forge.
