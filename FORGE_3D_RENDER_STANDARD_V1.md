# Forge 3D Form Demo Render Standard v1

## Delivery
- Primary format: WebM.
- 30 fps.
- Target loop: 3–5 seconds.
- Square framing optimized for the Forge mobile Form Guide.
- Side view is primary; front or 3/4 front is secondary.
- Camera remains fixed during a teaching loop.
- Muted; no audio dependency.

## Character
Use one consistent neutral Forge athlete rig. The silhouette must make shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles readable on a phone. Avoid exaggerated bodybuilding proportions that hide joint paths.

## Equipment
Equipment must match the named exercise. Bars, benches, racks, cables, machines, handles and plates must visibly interact with the athlete rather than floating independently.

## Motion
A loop should show the true start position, controlled eccentric/working phase, brief readable end position, and controlled return. Do not use whole-character translation as a substitute for joint animation.

## Camera
Choose an angle that exposes the important technique. Side view is default for sagittal-plane movements. Secondary view should reveal alignment that the primary view cannot.

## Review
A 3D demo cannot be marked Reviewed until both views exist and the in-app checks pass: correct exercise, equipment interaction, range of motion, joint path, primary view, secondary view, loop quality and mobile testing.
