Forge Fitness v14.27.2 — Google Calendar Quick Setup

1. Open START_BACKEND.bat in Notepad.

2. Replace:
   PASTE_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID_HERE
   PASTE_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET_HERE

3. Keep this redirect URI exactly as written:
   http://127.0.0.1:8000/me/calendar/google/callback

4. In Google Cloud Console, add the same value under:
   OAuth Client -> Authorized redirect URIs

5. Save START_BACKEND.bat.

6. Close the currently running Forge backend CMD window and start
   START_BACKEND.bat again.

7. In Forge:
   Settings -> Training Settings -> Calendar & Time

8. The Google Calendar card should say "Ready to connect".
   Press "Connect Google Calendar".

9. Sign into the Google account you want Forge to use and approve access.

If the card still says "Google setup needed", press "Recheck Google Setup".
The card will list the exact missing environment variable(s).
