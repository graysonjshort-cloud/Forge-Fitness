# Forge Fitness v14.9 — Sport-Specific Training

Adds a persistent Sport setting during onboarding and in Training Settings.

Sports include General Fitness, Football, Basketball, Soccer, Baseball, Hockey,
Tennis, Volleyball, Wrestling, Combat Sports, Track/Sprinting, Distance Running,
Swimming, and Lacrosse.

Sport selection now affects:
- automatic workout split selection
- exercise scoring/selection
- target muscle emphasis
- rep-range bias
- conditioning exercise selection when room is available

Manual Workout Split still overrides the sport-recommended split.
